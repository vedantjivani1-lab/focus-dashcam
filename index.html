Before your first drive

Get a stand.
It matters.

Focus can only record what your phone can see. A phone lying on the dashboard or sliding around a cup holder records the ceiling, the floor or a blur. A sturdy stand is what turns your phone into a proper dashcam.

01 · You have this

Your phone

Any iPhone or Android phone with Focus installed. The camera does the recording, and the motion sensors watch for crashes.

02 · Important

A sturdy stand

A windshield or dashboard phone mount with a firm grip. It's the one thing that makes the biggest difference:

  • Keeps the camera pointed at the road for the whole drive
  • Less shake, so video is steadier and number plates are easier to read
  • Fewer false crash alerts from a phone that wobbles or falls
  • Your hands stay on the wheel, not on the phone
03 · Recommended

A car charger

Plug in on longer drives and your phone stays topped up, so Focus keeps recording all the way home.

FAQ

Before you
hit the road

The questions people ask before paying ₹1 a day. Something else? Write to genzdigitalprivatelimited@gmail.com.

Should I keep my phone charging?

For longer drives, yes. Keeping your phone on a car charger means it stays topped up and Focus records the whole trip, however long the road.

Do I need a phone stand?

Yes, we strongly recommend one. A firm windshield or dashboard mount keeps the camera aimed at the road, gives steadier video and clearer number plates, and avoids false crash alerts from a phone that moves around. Focus works without one, but you'll get much better footage with it.

Will my phone heat up?

Phones get warm when they record for a long time, especially in direct sun on the dashboard. Mount yours where the AC reaches it and out of direct sunlight. If a phone gets too hot, iOS or Android may slow it down or pause the camera to protect it.

How much storage does it use?

Only as much as you allow. You set a storage limit in Settings, and when Focus reaches it, it records over the oldest footage.

Locked crash clips are never recorded over; they stay until you delete them. Pick 720p to keep more footage, or 1080p for sharper detail.

Will the police and my insurer accept the footage?

Dashcam footage is often used to support insurance claims and police complaints, but whether it is accepted in a particular case is up to the insurer, the police or the court.

Focus helps by writing the time, speed and GPS location into the video and locking crash clips so they can't be overwritten. Keep the original file and share a copy.

iPhone or Android?

Both. Focus has the same features on iPhone and Android at the same ₹1 a day (₹30 a month). You pay through the App Store or Google Play, depending on your phone.

Does Focus record audio?

No, never. Focus records video only.

Will crash detection always catch a real crash?

No. It works on a motion-sensor threshold and isn't a certified crash detector, so it can miss a real impact or trigger on a pothole. In a real emergency, always call 112 yourself.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, from your subscriptions in the App Store or Google Play. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
